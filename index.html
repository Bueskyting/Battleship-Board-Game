Bot (CPU)

Enemy Fleet

Rules:

  • Place all 9 vessels on your side of the board (or hit Random)
  • Vessels cannot be placed next to each other, not even diagonally!
  • Misplaced one? Use Undo or Clear
  • Take turns firing. A hit lets you fire again
  • When a vessel sinks, the water around it is marked automatically
  • Sink the whole enemy fleet to win

Controls:

R - Rotates your vessel

LMB - Places a vessel / fires a shot

RMB - Flag an empty spot

- Missed shot

- Target was hit

- Vessel sunk

- Flagged as empty
